# Store listing copy (2026-10-09)

Written ahead of the Play testers' report, from what they flagged on bekin
and direct democracy: the description was too short to rank in search and
had no feature list, and the screenshots didn't show the features. The live
App Store description today is one sentence. Limits are noted per field and
every field below fits.

Where each field lives (same paths as direct democracy's store-listing.md):
- Google Play Console: your app → Grow users → Store presence → Main store
  listing. "Short description" and "Full description" are there.
- App Store Connect, "Subtitle": your app → App Information (left sidebar,
  under General) → Localizable Information → Subtitle. Saved separately and
  goes live with the next version.
- App Store Connect, "Promotional Text", "Description", "Keywords": your
  app → iOS App → the version you are preparing, under the screenshots.
  Promotional text can change any time; description and keywords change
  only with a version.

Keywords worked in: habit tracker, habits, streaks, routine, goals, notes,
checklist, to do, chores, steps, meditation, journal, self improvement,
discipline, stoic.

Track habits with one tap, take quick notes with tags and checklists, and keep chores from slipping. Built to get you in and back out to your life.

## Full description (4000 max, both stores)

Escape from Hades IRL is a habit tracker, notes app, and chore list built around one idea: fewer taps, so you can get back to your life. Your bad habits are the underworld. Every day you show up is a step out.

HABITS IN ONE TAP
Each habit is a tile. Tap it to log today. Track yes or no habits, or count things like pages read or glasses of water, with optional bronze, silver, and gold goals. See your week at a glance, and a monthly heatmap that shows how each day went when you tap it.

STEPS AND MEDITATION
Pull your daily steps straight from Apple Health or Health Connect, no typing. Run a meditation timer that logs itself when it finishes.

QUESTS
Long-term challenges for walking, meditating, writing, reading, exercise, or anything you make up. Each quest is tied to a habit and shows where you stand over the last 30 days and the last 18 months, so one bad week doesn't erase a good year.

NOTES THAT KEEP UP
Open straight to a fresh note and start typing. Tag notes, pin them, turn lines into checklists, and undo or redo as you write. Word suggestions learn your own vocabulary. Export your notes in one go to paste into an AI or keep as a backup.

CHORES
See how long it's been since you last cleaned the fridge or changed the sheets, and which chores are late or coming due. Add one-time tasks with a due date and check them off.

WISDOM ALONG THE WAY
Stay with it and you'll earn a quote from Marcus Aurelius, Epictetus, Seneca, and others, one for every ten days you show up.

DARK BY DEFAULT
It's hell. It's dark. A light mode is there if you want it.

YOUR DATA
Sign in with Apple, Google, or email. Your habits and notes sync across your devices. Delete your account and every bit of your data from inside the app, any time.
